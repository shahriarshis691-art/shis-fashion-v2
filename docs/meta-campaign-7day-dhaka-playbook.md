# SHIS Fashion - 7 Day Meta Campaign Playbook (Dhaka Focus)

Date: 2026-07-27  
Primary Goal: More purchases from youth audience in Dhaka for Oversized Tee, Denim, Panjabi.  
Secondary Goal: Build retargeting pool and improve best-seller velocity.

## 1) Campaign Objective and Setup

1. Campaign objective: Sales (Purchase optimized).
2. Geography priority:
- Tier 1: Dhaka (major budget)
- Tier 2: Chattogram, Sylhet, Khulna, Rajshahi (support budget)
3. Age focus:
- Oversized Tee, Denim: 18-30
- Panjabi: 20-35
4. Placement: Advantage+ placements (then trim poor placements after 3-4 days).
5. Attribution: 7-day click, 1-day view.

## 2) Budget Split (Initial)

Use this split for day 1 to day 3, then rebalance:

1. Prospecting: 60%
2. Retargeting (VC/ATC/IC): 30%
3. Existing customer or warm audience upsell: 10%

Dhaka city budget allocation inside each campaign:

1. Dhaka only ad set: 70%
2. Other major cities ad set: 30%

## 3) Campaign Structure

Create separate campaigns or ad sets by product line:

1. Oversized Tee
2. Denim
3. Panjabi

Inside each:

1. Prospecting ad set:
- Broad + age cap + Dhaka location
- Interest stack (fashion, streetwear, denim, lifestyle)
2. Retargeting ad set:
- 7-day ViewContent
- 14-day AddToCart
- 30-day InitiateCheckout without purchase
3. High intent ad set:
- Product page viewers of best sellers
- Cart abandoners

## 4) Website Changes Required Before Day 1

1. Dedicated landing pages:
- Oversized Tee collection page

2. Above-the-fold trust line:

4. Product page:

## 7) 7 Day Execution Plan

## Day 1 - Launch Baseline

1. Launch all 3 product campaigns.
2. Keep 2 to 3 creatives per ad set.
3. QA events using test flow once after launch.
4. Track hourly for first 6 hours.

Success markers:

1. No missing purchase funnel events
2. Stable landing page load on mobile

## Day 2 - Early Optimization

1. Pause ads with low CTR and high CPC.
2. Keep top 1 to 2 creatives per ad set.
3. Increase retargeting budget if ATC volume is strong.

Success markers:

1. CTR improving
2. AddToCart rate improving

## Day 3 - Landing Page Tightening

1. Update hero copy for top-performing product line.
2. Reorder page sections to show best sellers first.
3. Add urgency text where stock is actually low.

Success markers:

1. ViewContent to AddToCart conversion up

## Day 4 - Offer Test

1. Introduce one limited-time offer:
- Combo offer for tee
- Free/discounted delivery threshold
2. Run A/B with no-offer control ad.

Success markers:

1. InitiateCheckout rate up
2. No profit collapse

## Day 5 - City Expansion Tuning

1. Compare Dhaka vs other major cities by CPA.
2. Move more budget to lower CPA ad set.
3. Keep Dhaka as primary unless CPA spikes.

Success markers:

1. Purchase CPA stable or lower

## Day 6 - Retargeting Push

1. Refresh retargeting creatives with social proof.
2. Add WhatsApp support line in ad copy.
3. Focus on 7-day website visitors and cart abandoners.

Success markers:

1. Retargeting ROAS improved

## Day 7 - Scale Decision Day

1. Scale winning ad sets by 15 to 25 percent only.
2. Keep losing ad sets paused.
3. Export weekly report and set next week plan.

Success markers:

1. Purchase volume up
2. CPA within target
3. Funnel drop-offs identified

## 8) Daily KPI Dashboard (Track Every Day)

1. Spend
2. CTR
3. CPC
4. Landing page views
5. ViewContent
6. AddToCart
7. InitiateCheckout
8. Purchase
9. Cost per Purchase
10. ROAS

Target logic:

1. If CTR low and CPC high: change hook and first 3 seconds of creative.
2. If VC high but ATC low: improve product page trust, sizing, offer clarity.
3. If ATC high but Purchase low: reduce checkout friction and reassure COD/exchange.

## 10) Go/No-Go Rules for Scaling

Go to scale if all conditions are true for 2 days:

1. Events are firing correctly
2. Purchase volume is stable
3. Cost per Purchase is within acceptable range
4. Top product line has clear winner creatives

No-Go if any condition is true:

1. Missing funnel events
2. Checkout errors or major drop at InitiateCheckout to Purchase
3. CPA spikes with no conversion lift

## 14) Simple Rule to Choose Budget Plan

1. New pixel or low purchase history: start with Plan A.
2. Some purchase history in last 30 days: start with Plan B.
3. Strong purchase history and stable funnel: start with Plan C.

## 15) When to Increase Budget

Increase daily budget by 15 to 20 percent only when all are true for 2 consecutive days:

1. Purchase event tracking is stable.
2. CPA is at or below your target.
3. Checkout-to-purchase drop-off is not rising.

## 16) Ultra-Low Budget Plan: 1,000 BDT/day (Oversized T-shirt Only)

Use this plan when you want to start lean and test signal quality first.

Daily split:

1. Prospecting: 700
2. Retargeting: 300

Geography split:

1. Dhaka: 800
2. Other major cities combined: 200

Campaign structure (keep simple):

1. One campaign objective: Sales (Purchase)
2. Product line: Oversized T-shirt only
3. Two ad sets:
- Ad set A: Prospecting (Broad, Dhaka-heavy)
- Ad set B: Retargeting (7-day ViewContent and 14-day AddToCart)

Creative setup:

1. Use only 2 creatives in prospecting:
- 1 short video (6 to 10 seconds)
- 1 static/carousel best-seller creative
2. Use 1 retargeting creative with trust copy:
- Fast delivery in Dhaka
- Cash on Delivery
- Easy size exchange

Bidding and optimization notes:

1. Keep Advantage+ placements on.
2. Do not duplicate ad sets during first 3 days.
3. Do not edit budget more than once per day.

Decision rules for this budget:

1. If no AddToCart in 48 hours:
- Replace first 3-second hook and main thumbnail.
2. If AddToCart exists but no Purchase in 72 hours:
- Improve product page trust block and checkout clarity.
3. If Purchase starts and CPA is acceptable for 2 days:
- Increase total budget from 1,000 to 1,200 to 1,300 (max 20 percent step).

Minimum tracking checks each day:

1. Meta: ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, Purchase
2. GA4: view_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout, purchase
